Simple C2C flow

How GreenHub Marketplace works

1

Join GreenHub

Choose €1.99 monthly or €19.99 yearly membership for marketplace participation.

2

List your plant

Add the plant details and choose Direct Sale (optionally also accepting exchange offers), Exchange-only, or Auction.

3

Match the right deal

Buy at the listed price, offer an already-listed plant for exchange, or bid in an active auction.

4

Agree & complete

Sales connect buyer and seller; exchanges require approval by both members; auctions run for a fixed 7-day period.
