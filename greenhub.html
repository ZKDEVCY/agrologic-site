How GreenHub Marketplace works

1

List your plant

Add photos, plant details, location and choose Sale, Exchange or Both.

2

Discover & decide

Browse community listings. Use AR where available to check how a plant fits your space.

3

Connect directly

Buyers contact sellers or submit a plant-for-plant exchange proposal.

4

Complete the deal

The parties agree on the transaction. Future GreenHub payments will support a small service fee on successful sales.

More than buying

Keep useful plants in circulation.

GreenHub is designed to make it easier for plants, cuttings, pups and surplus specimens to move between people instead of being discarded.
